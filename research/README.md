Functional Psychology — Research Program

Author

Vladimir Gulyaev

This section describes the public research program of Functional Psychology.

The purpose of the research layer is to transform theoretical propositions, diagnostic observations and working hypotheses into testable scientific questions.

Functional Psychology distinguishes clearly between:

* canonical definitions of the system
* theoretical models
* diagnostic observations
* working hypotheses
* empirically supported findings
* independently replicated findings

⸻

Research Principle

The existence of a coherent theory is not the same as empirical validation.

The research objective is therefore:

Theory
↓
Operationalization
↓
Measurement
↓
Testing
↓
Replication
↓
Scientific Evaluation

The system should become increasingly testable rather than increasingly protected from falsification.

⸻

Main Research Domains

1. Functional Psychotypes

Research questions include:

* Can A, B, C and D be operationalized reliably?
* Can independent diagnosticians identify the same underlying psychotype?
* How stable is psychotype classification over time?
* Can psychotype be distinguished from role, skill and temporary state?
* Do proposed psychotypes predict meaningful behavioral differences?

⸻

2. Functional Vectors

The system proposes two complementary functional vectors:

1 → A / B
0 → C / D

Future research may examine whether the proposed orientations toward:

* expansion
* development
* stabilization
* maintenance

can be measured independently and whether they provide explanatory value beyond existing psychological models.

⸻

3. E — Disintegration

A central theoretical proposition is:

A / B / C / D = underlying functional architecture
E = disintegration state

Research questions include:

* Can disintegration be measured independently from psychotype?
* Does E show distinct functional patterns across A–D?
* Can local and systemic disintegration be distinguished reliably?
* Can movement toward integration be measured longitudinally?

⸻

4. Diagnostic Reliability

Functional Diagnostics requires formal reliability testing.

Potential studies include:

* inter-rater reliability
* test–retest stability
* diagnostic agreement
* effects of diagnostician training
* analysis of disagreement cases
* comparison of human and AI-assisted diagnosis

⸻

5. Construct Validity

Functional Psychology should be compared with established constructs.

Potential comparison areas include:

* Big Five personality dimensions
* temperament
* attachment
* cognitive style
* motivation
* stress regulation
* interpersonal behavior

The scientific question is not only whether correlations exist, but whether Functional Psychology provides additional explanatory or predictive value.

⸻

6. Predictive Validity

Prospective studies may examine whether Functional Psychology predicts:

* decision patterns
* relationship dynamics
* leadership behavior
* responses to uncertainty
* organizational behavior
* stress reactions
* developmental trajectories

Predictions should ideally be specified before outcomes are observed.

⸻

7. Interaction Between Psychotypes

A major research direction concerns the principle that important psychological processes emerge between functional architectures.

Potential research questions include:

* Do different pairings produce reproducible interaction patterns?
* Can complementarity be measured?
* Can conflict loops be predicted?
* Does interaction with different psychotypes expand behavioral repertoire?
* How do verbal and nonverbal processes differ across pairings?

⸻

8. Organizational Systems

Functional Psychology proposes the high-level organizational model:

A → creation / expansion
B → development
C → stabilization / mastery
D → maintenance

Research may test:

* whether organizations show distinguishable functional stages
* whether dominant function predicts organizational behavior
* whether functional mismatch predicts stagnation or instability
* whether reactivation of development functions predicts renewal
* how team composition interacts with system stage

⸻

9. Development

A central proposition is:

development ≠ psychotype replacement

Research should examine:

* stability of underlying psychotype
* expansion of functional repertoire
* changes produced by learning
* psychotherapy
* leadership experience
* life transitions
* long-term adaptation

⸻

10. Compensation and Reconstruction

Future studies may investigate:

* primary vs. secondary compensation
* cost of compensatory functioning
* stability of compensation
* transition from compensation toward integration
* reconstructive change over time

⸻

Working Hypotheses

Some propositions within Functional Psychology remain explicitly classified as working hypotheses.

These may include hypotheses concerning:

* sex-linked distribution of psychotypes
* family structures
* reproductive patterns
* characteristic stress responses
* developmental origins
* organizational dominance
* specific physiological correlates

These should not be presented as established facts until appropriate evidence exists.

⸻

Sex-Linked Model

The current working model describes:

A / D → male functional forms
B / C → female functional forms

This remains a working hypothesis requiring systematic empirical investigation.

Possible research areas include:

* prevalence
* exceptions
* developmental influences
* biological correlates
* cultural effects
* alternative explanations

⸻

Research Methodology

The scientific program should progressively incorporate methods such as:

* preregistered hypotheses
* blinded assessment
* independent raters
* structured coding
* comparison groups
* prospective prediction
* longitudinal observation
* cross-cultural samples
* replication

⸻

Evidence Ladder

A useful model for scientific development is:

Canonical Concept
↓
Repeated Observation
↓
Working Hypothesis
↓
Operationalization
↓
Controlled Testing
↓
Replication
↓
Accumulated Evidence

Different propositions within Functional Psychology may currently occupy different levels of this ladder.

⸻

Negative Results

Negative findings are scientifically valuable.

If evidence contradicts a hypothesis, possible outcomes include:

* revising the hypothesis
* narrowing its scope
* identifying boundary conditions
* rejecting the hypothesis

The research program should not require every original proposition to survive unchanged.

⸻

AI-Assisted Research

AI may assist future research through:

* structured case coding
* hypothesis comparison
* semantic analysis
* multilingual research
* inter-rater comparison
* longitudinal pattern analysis
* consistency checks

However:

AI output ≠ empirical validation

An AI trained on Functional Psychology cannot independently prove Functional Psychology correct.

⸻

Scientific Collaboration

Functional Psychology is intended to remain open to:

* scientific examination
* critique
* comparison
* replication
* methodological discussion
* independent research

Scientific collaboration does not require prior agreement with the theory.

The purpose is to test it.

⸻

Core Research Questions

The long-term research program can be summarized through several fundamental questions:

Can the proposed architectures be measured?
Can independent observers identify them?
Are they stable over time?
Do they predict behavior?
Do they explain something not already explained by existing models?
Can the predictions be independently replicated?
